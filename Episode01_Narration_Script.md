# Episode 01 — Narration Script ("The Light in Your Pocket")
*For ElevenLabs (free tier). Export as `narration.mp3` → drop into `ep/01/`.*

---

## Voice & settings

- **Voice:** a warm, gentle storyteller — unhurried, kind, a little hushed (like reading a bedtime story). Good ElevenLabs picks: a soft older narrator, or a warm calm female voice. Pick ONE and reuse it for the whole series.
- **Model:** Multilingual v2 (best quality).
- **Stability:** ~50%  ·  **Similarity:** ~75%  ·  **Style:** low (0–15%)  ·  **Speaker boost:** on.
- **Pace:** slow. Let the pauses breathe — the silences are part of the mood.

---

## Paste THIS into ElevenLabs (clean — ellipses/line breaks create the pauses)

```
Every light starts the same way. Small. Uncertain. Yours.

This is Fenn. His lantern went out... a long time ago.

He's been waiting. Not for a hero... for you.

So light it. Go on...

The whole world's been holding its breath for this.
```

## How it lines up with the app

- Narration starts **after** the intro video + the black beat (once the dark forest emerges).
- "So light it. Go on…" (0:16) invites the tap. If the child hasn't tapped by ~0:20, the lantern **auto-lights** on the last line — so it always pays off.
- The captions appear on a timer. **If your recording's pacing differs, either:** (a) match these times when you record, or (b) send me the finished `narration.mp3` and I'll measure it and set the exact caption times for you.
