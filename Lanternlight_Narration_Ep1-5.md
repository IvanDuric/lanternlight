# Lanternlight — Narration Scripts (Episodes 1–5)
*One warm storyteller voice for the whole series. Record each, name it `narration.mp3`, drop it in that episode's folder (`ep/01/`, `ep/02/`, …). Send them to me and I'll fit them and sync the captions.*

**ElevenLabs settings (same for all):** Multilingual v2 · Stability ~50% · Similarity ~75% · Style low · slow, gentle pacing. Let the pauses breathe.

---

## Episode 1 — Fenn the Foxlight  *(already recorded)*
```
Every light starts the same way. Small. Uncertain. Yours.
This is Fenn. His lantern went out... a long time ago.
He's been waiting. Not for a hero... for you.
So light it. Go on...
The whole world's been holding its breath for this.
```

## Episode 2 — Momo the Moth-Bear  ·  "The Bear Who Ate Worries"
```
Some creatures carry more than they ever show.
Momo swallows everyone's worries, so no one else has to.
But nobody ever thinks to carry Momo's.
So breathe with it. In... and slowly out.
There. Let them go.
```
*Delivery: soft and calming, like helping someone settle. The last line on an exhale.*

## Episode 3 — Tuck & Tally, the Ink-Otters  ·  "Mapmakers of the Ink River"
```
Tuck and Tally know every secret bend of the Ink River.
But their map is torn clean in two — and neither one will share.
A map is really just a story about where you're brave enough to go.
Bring the two halves together...
and watch the river remember its way.
```
*Delivery: playful, a little mischievous, warming to wonder at the end.*

## Episode 4 — Silva the Owl  ·  "The Almanac of Forgotten Things"
```
Silva keeps the Almanac — the book of everything the world is trying not to forget.
But the pages are going blank, one memory at a time.
The Hush doesn't roar. It erases. Quietly.
So help her remember.
Some things are worth staying awake a hundred years for.
```
*Delivery: hushed, wise, a librarian's calm; a little sad on "erases," hopeful at the end.*

## Episode 5 — Bram the Beetle  ·  "The Firefly Forge"
```
Every Sparkstone begins here, in Bram's forge.
But the fire has burned down to a single, stubborn ember.
You can't hurry a light into being.
So give it a spark. And then another.
Slow... is not the same as stopped.
```
*Delivery: warm and patient, like an old craftsman; steady rhythm building to the last line.*

## After you record

1. Save each as `narration.mp3` in its episode folder and send them to me.
2. I'll measure each recording and **sync the on-screen captions to it** (Episodes 2–5 currently show captions on a timer; with real audio I'll switch them to follow the voice, like Episode 1).
3. If a line's wording changes as you record, no problem — send what you have and I'll match the captions to it.
