# Lanternlight — Timed Narration (Prologue + Episodes 2–5)
*Episode 1 is already recorded. One warm storyteller voice for all of these.*

## How to keep it natural (not robotic)
- **`…`** = short breath, ~0.5 s.  **line break** = a real pause, ~1 s.  **`[[hold]]`** = a longer beat, ~1.5–2 s.
- In ElevenLabs you can also type `<break time="1.0s" />` where you want a set pause.
- Speak **slowly**. Let the end of each sentence settle before the next. Warm, hushed, like a bedtime story.
- Settings: Multilingual v2 · Stability ~50% · Similarity ~75% · Style low.

Save each as `narration.mp3` in its folder (`ep/02/`, …); the prologue as `prologue.mp3`. Send them and I'll drop them in and sync the captions to your voice.
